Help

How is my fare worked out?

A base fare plus a rate per kilometre and per minute for the road route between your pickup and drop. You see the exact fare before you book.

How do I pay?

Pay the driver by cash or UPI once your goods are delivered. The driver shows a UPI QR code for the exact amount.

What is the delivery OTP?

For your safety the driver needs a 4-digit code to finish the delivery. It's sent to the receiver by SMS and shown on your tracking screen.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge, any time before the driver picks up your goods. Open the booking and tap Cancel booking.

What can't I send?

Anything illegal, hazardous or flammable, cash and valuables, and live animals.
